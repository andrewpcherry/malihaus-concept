What this actually looks like.

Questions people in this situation actually ask.

Is it too late once the lender has filed?

Usually not. A Florida foreclosure is a court process and it takes time. What is true is that the options narrow as it goes on, so the earlier you know what the house is worth, the more of them you still have.

Will this hurt my credit more than the foreclosure would?

That is a question for a housing counsellor or an attorney rather than for a buyer, and anyone who tells you otherwise while trying to buy your house is selling you something. We will not pretend to know your situation.

What if I owe more than the house is worth?

Then a straight sale may not clear it, and you need to know that before you spend energy on it. Tell us the balance early and we will tell you honestly whether any of the three routes gets you out clean.

Do I have to pay anything to find out?

No. No repairs, no fees, no commission, and nothing owed if you look at the number and walk away.

Can I stay in the house while this is happening?

Yes, and you choose the closing date, so you are not being moved out on someone else's schedule.
