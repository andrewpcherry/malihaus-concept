What this actually looks like.

The house has probably been sitting. Nobody has run the air conditioning properly, the roof is whatever age it is, and the last serious work on it happened a long time ago. That is normal for this. It does not change whether we will buy it.

What usually matters more than the condition is that everyone entitled to a share needs to agree, and that takes as long as it takes. We can hold a number in place while that gets sorted out.

We are not attorneys and nothing here is legal advice. If the estate is in probate, your attorney decides what can be signed and when. Our job is to give you a real number so the family is deciding with actual figures instead of guesses.

Questions people in this situation actually ask.

Can you buy the house before probate is finished?

Often the sale can be set up while probate is still running, but whether it can actually close depends on the court and on how the estate was left. That is a question for the estate's attorney. What we can do is put a number in front of you that stays good while you find out.

Do we have to clear the house out first?

No. Take what the family wants and leave the rest. Sorting through a lifetime of a house is the part people dread most, and it is not a condition of us buying.

There is still a mortgage on it. Does that stop this?

No. A mortgage gets paid off out of the sale at closing, the same as any other sale. If the balance is close to what the house is worth, tell us early, because that changes which of the three routes makes sense.

Several of us inherited it and we do not agree. Can you still help?

We can give you a number, which is usually the thing the disagreement is actually about. We cannot make the decision for you and we will not push anyone. When everyone is ready, we are ready.

Will you tell us if we should just list it instead?

Yes. We run every house against all three routes before anyone quotes you anything, and if listing it on the open market beats our own offer we say so, even though that means we are not the ones buying it.
