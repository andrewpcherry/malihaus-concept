What this actually looks like.

A real offer settles that. It gives both sides and both attorneys one figure to work from instead of two opinions, and it can be done without a single showing.

Questions people in this situation actually ask.

Will this show up on the public listing sites?

Not if you do not want it to. Both the direct purchase and the private renovation buyer route happen without a public listing, so nothing appears on the portals.

We do not agree on what the house is worth. Can you help with that?

That is usually the real problem, and a written offer is the most useful thing anyone can hand you, because it turns an argument into a figure. Both sides get the same number at the same time.

Do we both have to sign?

Whoever is on the title has to sign. If you are not sure who that is or what your agreement requires, ask your attorney before anything else.

Can we time the closing around the court dates?

Yes. You choose the closing date, which is one of the main reasons this route suits divorces at all.

What if one of us wants to keep the house?

Then do not sell it. We will still give you the number, because knowing what it is worth is useful whether you are selling it or buying the other person out of it.
