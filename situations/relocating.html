Questions people in this situation actually ask.

I have already moved out of state. Can I still do this?

Yes. This gets handled remotely all the time. Every purchase closes at a local title company, and they are set up for sellers who are not in the room.

Can I close after I move, or before?

Either. You choose the closing date. That is usually the single most useful part of this for anyone relocating.

The house is empty. Does that make it harder?

No. An empty house is easier for us, not harder, and it is the situation where carrying costs are quietly doing the most damage to you.

Should I just rent it out instead?

Sometimes that is genuinely the better answer, particularly if there is a low rate on the mortgage. We are not going to talk you out of it. Get the number first so you are comparing two real options.
