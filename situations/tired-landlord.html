Questions people in this situation actually ask.

Do I have to evict the tenant first?

No. We buy with the tenant in place. If anything, on the national network an occupied property with a paying tenant is worth more, not less.

What happens to my tenant after the sale?

The lease goes with the property, so their agreement carries on. We are not in the business of turning up on day one and putting somebody out.

The tenant has damaged the place. Does that kill it?

No. We buy as it stands and we are not asking you to repair anything. Tell us what the damage actually is so the number we give you is the number that holds.

Can I sell more than one at a time?

Yes. If you are winding a small portfolio down, send the whole list. It is usually easier to price several together than one at a time.

What do you need from me to start?

The address, the rent, and roughly where the lease sits. That is enough to get you a real answer.
