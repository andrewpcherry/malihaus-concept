01  /  Missed calls

A missed call becomes a text message before they dial the next buyer.

A seller in the middle of something calls two or three companies in one evening and goes with whoever answers. Right now, when you are on a walkthrough or asleep, that call becomes a voicemail nobody listens to.

The moment a call to your number goes unanswered, a text goes out automatically and the conversation carries on without you. If the seller replies, it keeps going.

  • Fires on every unanswered call, day or night, weekends included
  • Comes from your own number, so replies land where you expect
  • Stops the moment a human joins the thread

03  /  Nothing goes cold

The ones who do not answer are worth more than the ones who do.

Most of the money in a list like yours is sitting in people who enquired months ago and were never chased, because chasing is boring and there is always something more urgent.

Follow up runs on its own, in a sequence that changes depending on what the seller told you. Somebody weeks from a foreclosure date is treated differently from somebody thinking about it next year. It stops the second they reply, and it never sends twice in a way that looks automated.

  • Different sequences for urgent, warm and long term
  • Text, email and voicemail, not the same channel over and over
  • Old leads reactivated from the list you already own, without a call block
  • Everything logged against the seller, so nothing is asked twice

05  /  Being found

Sellers now ask an assistant before they open a search page.

Somebody types "how do I sell an inherited house in Florida without doing repairs" into ChatGPT and gets an answer built out of pages that address that exact question in plain language.

Your site currently has nothing for that to draw on, and ten footer links that all lead back to the same Boca Raton page. That gets rebuilt as real pages, one per situation, each written for one circumstance and each ending in the same place.

  • Your Google listing corrected and completed, with the right categories
  • One phone number everywhere instead of the three currently in public
  • Situation pages marked up so assistants can quote them
  • Real market pages for the counties you actually work

06  /  What you see

Right now none of this is measured, which is the part that limits everything else.

There is no analytics, no tag manager, no advertising pixel and no call tracking anywhere on malihaus.com today. So there is no way to know how many people arrive, what they do, or what a lead costs. That goes in first, before anything else is changed, so that every change after it can be judged against a real number.

One report, every week
•Visits and where from
•Calls, answered and missed
•Conversations started
•Appointments booked
•New reviews
•Cost per lead

Blank because there is nothing to fill them with yet. That is the point.

None of this asks anything of you once it is running.

You keep doing the part you are good at, which is looking at houses and making offers. The rest of it answers, asks, writes it down, books the time and tells you what happened.
