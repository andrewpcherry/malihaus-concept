01The phone

Every call gets answered, including the ones you cannot take.

Two things sit on your number. The first is missed call text back: the instant a call goes unanswered, a text goes out and the conversation carries on without you.

The second is a voice agent that actually answers. It picks up in a human sounding voice, asks about the property, and books straight into your calendar. It does not read a menu and it does not take a message.

  • Fires on every unanswered call, nights, weekends, holidays
  • Comes from your own number, so replies land where you expect
  • Every call tracked and recorded, so you know which ones came from where
  • Stops the second a human joins the thread

06The other half of your business

Everything above gets houses in. This is the side that gets them back out.

You run properties through your own capital, through local renovators and through national buyer platforms. That means a buyer list, and a buyer list is worth exactly as much as your ability to reach the right part of it quickly.

The same system holds your buyers as well as your sellers, and it knows which of them buys what. A new property goes out only to the ones who buy that zip code, that price band and that condition, by text and email at the same time, and you see who opened it and who is asking questions while the message is still going out.

  • Buyers segmented by market, price band, condition and how they pay
  • A property goes out in one action, to the right slice, not the whole list
  • You see who opened, who clicked and who replied, in order
  • Assignment paperwork signed on a phone the same way the purchase agreement was
  • Buyers who never take anything drop out of the list on their own

This is also the answer to the slowest part of a wholesale deal, which is not finding the house. It is working out which twelve people out of four hundred actually want it.
