## Implemented in the page source

- Reuses the existing MaliHaus HighLevel external-form intake and its public tracking ID. No private credentials are in the page.
- Native standard fields plus the existing exact seller custom-field labels. Property details, contact permission and campaign attribution are retained in Conversation Summary.
- Captures UTMs, GCLID, GBRAID, WBRAID and campaign/ad-group/keyword parameters in memory for the enquiry. No personal details or click IDs enter the page's analytics events.
- Final submission revalidates all three steps. Buttons and accepted values are locked during delivery. Multiple submit attempts share one delivery promise.
- Success requires a matching provider acknowledgement. HTTP failure, a negative acknowledgement or an uncertain timeout cannot produce success. A replay after uncertainty does not send again.
- A same-session fingerprint suppresses duplicate delivery for 24 hours. Only the hash, status and timestamp are stored. This is not cross-device deduplication.
- Enquiry measurement follows acknowledgement and suppresses repeated callbacks. Phone clicks remain separate events.
- Google measurement supports a verified GTM container, or direct GA4/Ads tags, with optional measurement choices. GTM and direct conversion tags are not both fired. Actual Google IDs are intentionally empty until the existing client accounts can be inspected.
- Delivery is restricted to the three configured MaliHaus HTTPS origins. Local/offline/source previews do not send enquiries.
