How quickly can I get a cash offer?

Start with the property details and your timing. We’ll review the information and discuss any inspection needed before making an offer. The timing depends on the property and access, so we won’t promise a deadline before reviewing it.

Do I have to make repairs or clean everything out?

You don’t need to renovate before we assess the house. Tell us about repairs, damage, or belongings you’d like to leave. Any cleanout arrangements will be discussed as part of the purchase terms.

How do you work out the offer?

We consider the property’s condition, needed repairs, local comparable sales, and the costs and risks of the project. A cash offer can be below a retail listing price. We’ll explain the proposed terms so you can decide whether the convenience fits your goals.

Will I pay fees or closing costs?

Requesting an offer is free. Any costs associated with the purchase should be set out in the offer and purchase agreement. Review those details before accepting, including any amounts that would be deducted from your proceeds.

Can I choose when I move out?

Tell us your preferred date early. We’ll discuss what is feasible and agree on the closing and possession terms in writing. Timing depends on the property, title, and agreed purchase terms.

Are you the buyer?

MaliHaus Capital LLC evaluates properties for direct cash purchases. Some properties may be a better fit for an investor partner or our investor network. We’ll explain who is buying and the proposed arrangement before you decide.

What happens after I send my details?

A member of the MaliHaus team contacts you to confirm the details, understand your timing, and discuss a property review. Submitting an enquiry doesn’t commit you to selling and doesn’t guarantee an offer.
