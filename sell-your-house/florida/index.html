Why consider MaliHaus?

Many buyers have one way of doing things. MaliHaus looks at the property first, then explains which of three selling routes may be open to you.

The right route depends on the property and on our purchase criteria. Sometimes none of them fits, and if that is the case we will tell you.

  1. 01

    A direct purchase

    If the property meets our buying criteria after an inspection, MaliHaus may buy it directly using its own funds.

  2. 02

    Improvements, then an investor partner

    For some properties, and only with your written approval, we may complete selected repairs while the property is under contract and bring in an investor partner to complete the purchase.

  3. 03

    Exposure to our investor network

    We can present the property to investors through a national real estate network, which can bring interest from beyond your local market.

No route is guaranteed. MaliHaus does not buy every property, and you are never obliged to accept what we put in front of you.

Practical questions

Do I need to repair or clean the property first?

No. We review the property in its current condition. You do not need to make repairs, clear it out or stage it before we look at it.

Can I enquire about an inherited house?

Yes. Tell us where things stand, for example whether the estate has been settled. Who is able to sign a sale depends on the estate, so we may suggest you confirm that with the attorney or court handling it. We cannot give legal advice.

Can I enquire about a property with tenants?

Yes. Tenanted properties are reviewed as they are. Let us know whether there is a lease in place and when it ends, because that affects which options are available.

How is an offer determined?

We look at the condition of the property, the repairs it needs, its location, what has sold nearby and which selling route fits. Any figure comes from that review. This page does not produce an automatic valuation, and an offer is not guaranteed.

How quickly could a sale happen?

It depends on the property, the title, the route chosen and the people involved, such as the title company, an investor partner, tenants or an estate. We do not promise a fixed number of days. If you have a date in mind, tell us and we will be straightforward about whether it looks realistic.

Does requesting an offer commit me to selling?

No. Requesting an offer only asks us to review the property and contact you. Nothing is binding unless you later choose to sign a written agreement.

Do you buy in my area?

We review properties in selected areas within Broward, Palm Beach, Duval (Jacksonville), Hillsborough (Tampa), Orange (Orlando), Seminole, Pasco and Polk counties. Being in one of those counties does not mean every address qualifies. Send us the property address and we will confirm whether it is in an area we are reviewing.

Will MaliHaus buy the property directly?

Sometimes. If the property meets our direct purchase criteria, MaliHaus may buy it itself. If not, one of the other two routes may apply: selected improvements with an investor partner, or exposure to our investor network. If none of the three fits, we will tell you.
